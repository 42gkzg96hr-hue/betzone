JetX Game: Rules & Cash-Out (and the truth about predictors)

JetX is a crash game similar to Aviator. Here are the rules, the cash-out, the RTP and the honest truth about the "predictors" sold everywhere.

1. What is JetX?

JetX (by SmartSoft) is a crash game similar to Aviator: a jet takes off, a multiplier rises (x1.1… x2… x5…) and can explode at any moment. Goal: cash out before the explosion. RTP around 97%. Available on 1win.

2. Rules & cash-out

  1. Place your bet before take-off (two bets possible).
  2. The multiplier climbs.
  3. Hit Cash Out to collect at the shown multiplier.
  4. If the jet explodes first, the bet is lost.

Like Aviator and Lucky Jet, JetX offers auto cash-out to avoid emotional decisions.

3. The truth about "predictors"

Let's be clear: no tool can predict the explosion. The result is random (RNG) and provably fair. "JetX predictor" apps, hacks and paid "signals" are scams. You only control your bet and your timing.

4. Honest tips

  • Set a budget and stop when it's reached.
  • A low auto cash-out (x1.4–x1.8) smooths risk.
  • Never chase losses.
  • Turn on a deposit limit.
