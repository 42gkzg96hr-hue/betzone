3. The REAL wagering requirements

This is what most beginners miss — and it costs them. A bonus comes with a wagering requirement: how many times you must re-bet the amount before you can withdraw.

Example: a $100 bonus with x40 wagering = $4,000 to bet before withdrawing bonus winnings. Also check:

  • The calculation base (wagering on bonus only, or deposit + bonus?).
  • The game contribution: slots often count 100%, blackjack/roulette 10% or 0%.
  • The time limit (often 7 days).
  • The max bet allowed while the bonus is active.

Simple rule: wagering ≤ x30 = fine; above x50 = skip it.
