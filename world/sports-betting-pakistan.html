Online betting is prohibited in Pakistan. Here is the legal position as it stands in 2026, the penalties involved, and where betting is regulated instead.

⚠️ Online betting is illegal in Pakistan

Under the Prevention of Gambling Act. Online betting is prohibited. Enforcement targets the mobile wallets used to fund it: reporting from 2025 describes JazzCash agents being detained and gambling-linked accounts frozen, with large transfers triggering automatic scrutiny.

Penalties: Criminal liability, plus freezing of the accounts used.
